Trusted Organisations & Helplines

Finding the right support should not be another challenge during a cancer journey. Explore established organisations, patient-support services and helplines that can help patients, survivors and caregivers find guidance, information and care.

Help Is Only a Conversation Away

Whether you need information about cancer care, emotional support, financial guidance or help navigating treatment, reaching out to an established organisation can be a useful first step.

This directory brings together organisations and contact services in India, including options relevant to people in Kerala. Contact details and service availability can change, so always confirm the latest information through the organisation's official website.

For Medical Emergencies

Cancer-specific helplines and support organisations are not emergency medical services. If someone is experiencing a life-threatening emergency, seek immediate medical attention rather than waiting for a support organisation to respond.

India Emergency Response Support System

112 – Emergency Helpline

For urgent cancer-treatment concerns, contact the treating hospital or oncology team directly.

Before Reaching Out

A few simple checks can help you find the right service and protect yourself while seeking assistance.

  • Verify contact details: Use the organisation's official website to confirm that a phone number or email address is current.
  • Ask what support is available: Services may include counselling, financial assistance, treatment guidance, palliative care or referrals.
  • Check eligibility: Some organisations provide services only in specific locations or to particular patient groups.
  • Protect your information: Do not share passwords, OTPs, banking PINs or unnecessary medical documents with unknown contacts.
  • Be careful with payment requests: Do not assume that a person requesting money through a private account represents an organisation.
  • Know the limits: Support organisations can provide guidance and assistance, but they do not replace your treating medical team.
Contact numbers, operating hours and services may change. UNMUTE provides this directory for informational purposes and does not guarantee the availability, eligibility or outcome of any service. Please confirm details directly with the organisation before relying on them.

Reaching Out Is a Strength

Asking for help can be difficult, but you do not have to figure everything out alone. Whether you need information, practical assistance or someone to speak with, the right support can help you take the next step.
