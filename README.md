# UNMUTE — Women's Cancer Information, Support & Research

**Making information accessible. Making support visible. Making every voice matter.**

UNMUTE is a web-based platform designed to bring together reliable women's cancer information, emotional and practical support, survivor stories, and research resources in one accessible space.

A cancer diagnosis can leave patients and their families navigating unfamiliar medical information, treatment decisions, emotional challenges, and uncertainty. Information and support are often scattered across different sources. UNMUTE aims to make that journey a little easier to navigate.

## About the Project

UNMUTE is designed to support people across different stages of the cancer journey — from understanding symptoms and diagnosis to navigating treatment, finding support, and exploring life beyond treatment.

The platform also aims to give greater visibility to underrepresented and less commonly discussed cancers, while encouraging informed awareness of ongoing research and clinical developments.

## Key Features

### 1. Know Your Body
An educational section that helps visitors understand women's cancers, their signs and symptoms, diagnostic processes, and available medical information.

### 2. Find Support
A collection of emotional, practical, financial, and community support resources for patients and caregivers.

### 3. Her Voice
A survivor-story section that allows visitors to explore stories through age and cancer-type filters.

**Demonstration content notice:** The survivor stories and portraits currently displayed in this section are fictional and AI-generated. They are created exclusively for demonstration purposes and are not real patient testimonials or medical evidence.

### 4. Beyond the Common
An expanding educational library focusing on rare, less common, and underrepresented cancers and cancer subtypes.

The initial guides cover:
- Ovarian clear cell carcinoma (OCCC)
- Triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC)

TNBC is a distinct breast cancer subtype, not technically classified as a rare disease.

### 5. Patient and Caregiver Resources
Dedicated resources addressing treatment support, emotional wellbeing, caregiver wellbeing, and practical ways to support someone living with cancer.

### 6. A Little Garden of Hope
A space intended to offer a moment of reflection, encouragement, and hope.

## Technology Stack

- **HTML5** — page structure and content

## Running the Project Locally

UNMUTE is a static website.

1. Clone the repository:

   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/nan-dana508/-UNMUTE.git
   ```

2. Open the project folder.

3. Open `index.html` in your browser.

Alternatively, open the project in Visual Studio Code and use the Live Server extension to preview it locally.

## Project Structure

The repository contains:

- `style.css` for shared website styling.

- Individual educational guides and survivor-story demonstration pages.

## Medical Information Disclaimer

UNMUTE is an educational and informational project. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Medical decisions should always be made in consultation with qualified healthcare professionals. Research findings and clinical-trial information should be interpreted in context, and eligibility and treatment availability may vary.

## Project Status

UNMUTE is being developed as a web project, with an initial set of educational and support resources. The platform is intended to grow through carefully reviewed content and additional resources over time.

## Vision

To create a space where people affected by women's cancers can find information, discover support, explore research, and feel less alone throughout their journey.
