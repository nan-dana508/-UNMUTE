Financial Support Shouldn't Be Out of Reach

A cancer diagnosis can bring financial challenges alongside the emotional and physical demands of treatment. Medical expenses, medicines, travel and everyday responsibilities can place a significant burden on patients and their families.

Understanding the assistance available can help make the financial side of cancer care feel more manageable. Explore government schemes, charitable support, hospital resources and practical guidance.

Important: UNMUTE provides general information about financial assistance and does not approve applications, provide grants or guarantee eligibility. You Deserve Support, Too

Financial difficulties should never make someone feel that they have to navigate cancer care alone. Asking questions, exploring assistance and reaching out for help are all valid steps. You deserve care, dignity and support throughout your journey.
